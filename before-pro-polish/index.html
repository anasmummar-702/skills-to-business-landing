He practised on his phone between deliveries for months and got nowhere. With a mentor checking his code every week, he built three live websites in four months and was hired by a Pune agency.

Marketing Homemaker → Freelance ads manager

Meera, 31

After a six-year career break she wanted work she could do from home. She learned Meta and Google Ads on her own small budget, then landed two local clients before the course even ended.

Coding B.Com graduate → Backend developer

Rohan, 22

Interviewers kept asking about projects he didn't have. His mentor helped him build and deploy a full booking system, and walking through that code is what got him his first offer.

Marketing Boutique owner → Doubled online orders

Sana, 28

Her shop's Instagram had followers but no sales. She learned content planning and paid campaigns in eight weeks, and now runs all of her shop's marketing herself.

Night shifts left him two hours a day to study. The mentor built his plan around those two hours, and in five months he moved into a day job building websites.

Marketing Fresher → SEO executive

Priya, 21

She sent fifty applications with nothing to show. After ranking a practice blog on Google's first page, she had real results to talk about, and the next interview turned into a job.
